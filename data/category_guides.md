# Electronics product guides

## Image requirements

Electronics products require clear, high-resolution primary images on a clean background. The primary image should show the complete product without obstruction. Additional images should highlight ports, accessories, labels, and other important features. Avoid misleading specifications and unrelated products.

## Return policy

Eligible products can be returned within 30 days if they meet return conditions. For fashion products, the item should be unused, unwashed, and returned with original tags and packaging intact.

## Warranty guidance

If a product stops working after the return window, buyers should check the product warranty and file a valid claim if the product is covered.
