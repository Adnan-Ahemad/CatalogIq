# Returns and replacement policy

## General return window

Eligible items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. The item must be in unused condition and should be returned with original packaging and tags intact. Items bought on sale are eligible if they meet the same return conditions.

## Fashion items

Fashion products should be unused, unwashed, and returned with original tags and packaging intact. If the product was bought on sale, it can generally be returned within 30 days subject to the same conditions.

## Warranty claims

After the regular return window expires, the buyer should check the product's stated warranty period and determine whether a warranty claim is available.
