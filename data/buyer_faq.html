Can I return a fashion item bought on sale?

Yes, eligible fashion products bought on sale can generally be returned within 30 days if they satisfy the return conditions.

How long can eligible fashion products be returned?

Eligible fashion products can generally be returned within 30 days of delivery if they are unused, unwashed, and returned with original tags and packaging intact.

What are the image requirements for electronics products?

Electronics products require clear high-resolution images, a complete primary product image on a clean background, additional detail images, and no misleading specifications or unrelated products.

What if a smartwatch from a third-party seller stops working after 45 days?

After the normal return window, check the product warranty and pursue an eligible warranty claim if applicable.
