# Seller guide

## Product listing image rules

Create a clear primary product image on a clean background. Include additional close-up images showing ports, accessories, labels, and features. Do not use misleading photography or unrelated products.

## Returns and buyer protection

If a product is purchased from a third-party seller and fails after the standard return window, the buyer should check the warranty coverage and file an eligible claim if the product qualifies.

## Warranty periods

Do not change the applicable warranty period because the seller is a third-party seller. The product's stated warranty remains the governing period.
